# Journey — Product Brief

## 1. Product statement

Journey is an experience-discovery platform concept that connects user-generated stories with actionable information about events, places, activities, and service providers.

## 2. Problem hypothesis

Discovery is fragmented. A person may see an attractive event or destination in a social post, but still need to search elsewhere for location, organiser, service details, cost, availability, and credibility.

**Hypothesis:** If discovery content is connected to structured experience and provider information, users can move from inspiration to an informed enquiry with less friction.

This is a hypothesis to validate, not a proven market finding.

## 3. Target users and jobs to be done

- **Explorer:** "When I want to do something interesting, help me discover relevant experiences and understand the details."
- **Creator:** "When I share an experience, help me attach useful context so others can discover it."
- **Provider / organiser:** "When people discover my service or event, help them understand what I offer and contact me."

## 4. Primary user journey

1. User opens Journey and selects a location or interest.
2. User browses a feed or category-based discovery page.
3. User opens an experience and reviews media, details, and source.
4. User views the linked organiser/provider profile.
5. User saves the experience or sends an enquiry.

## 5. MVP success criteria

Track whether users can complete the core discovery loop:
- Discovery-to-detail click-through rate.
- Percentage of detail views that result in a save or enquiry.
- Enquiry completion rate.
- Provider profile completeness.
- Percentage of listings with required location and source information.

Set numeric targets only after baseline testing.

## 6. Trust and safety requirements

- Label sponsored content clearly.
- Do not imply a provider is verified until checks are completed.
- Show who submitted or manages an experience listing.
- Provide report and moderation pathways.
- Avoid publishing private contact details without consent.
- Give providers a way to correct inaccurate information.
- Treat reviews and experience posts as user-generated content, not guaranteed endorsements.

## 7. Risks and open questions

- How will Journey prevent fake listings and misleading media?
- What evidence is sufficient for provider verification?
- Should initial discovery be city-first, category-first, or personalised?
- How will Journey moderate content and resolve provider disputes?
- What is the best initial niche or launch geography?
- Should enquiries happen in-app or through a provider's preferred channel?

## 8. MVP non-goals

The first release should not attempt to build a full social network, universal ticketing engine, complex payment orchestration, or AI recommendation system. Validate the discovery-to-enquiry loop first.
